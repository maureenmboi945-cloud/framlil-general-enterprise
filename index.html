ABOUT FRAMLIL

Growing Connections. Creating Opportunities.

Framlil General Enterprise Ltd. is an agricultural enterprise based in Thika, Kenya. We work with farmers and produce buyers to create reliable connections within the agricultural supply chain.

Our focus is on quality produce, dependable relationships and creating opportunities for farmers to reach wider markets.

Managed by Edwin Mwangi

Hass Avocado Size Guide

Hass avocado sizes are commonly represented by numbers. A lower number means a larger fruit.

14 Large
16 Large
18 Medium-Large
20 Medium
22 Medium
24 Medium
26 Smaller
